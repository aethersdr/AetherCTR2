# Credits

## SmartMTR, by Dariusz Socha, SQ5X

AetherCTR2's user-interface design is based on **SmartMTR** by
**Dariusz Socha, SQ5X**: https://smartmtr.net/

That covers the face's S-meter, gauges, layout and styling, laid out for the
CTR2-Max's screen, and the meter extremes engine (the peak and trough
markers). They are used with his permission, on the condition that he is
credited and his website is linked. That credit stays with every release.

## The CTR2-Max, by Lynn Hansen, Lynovation

The **CTR2-Max** controller, its hardware design and its stock firmware are
the work of **Lynn Hansen** of **Lynovation, LLC**: https://lynovation.com/

AetherCTR2 is distributed with Lynovation's authorization, runs beside the
stock firmware without changing it or its settings, and runs only on units
registered with it. Registration keys come from Lynovation. That credit
stays with every release.

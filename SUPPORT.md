# Getting Help with AetherCTR2

- **Installing, updating and switching firmware:** AetherSDR does all of it
  from **Settings → Controllers → CTR2...**. The [user guide](USER-GUIDE.md)
  walks through it.
- **A problem with AetherCTR2:** open an
  [issue](https://github.com/aethersdr/AetherCTR2/issues). Include the
  AetherCTR2 version (shown on its waiting screen), the AetherSDR version and
  its log, the radio model and firmware, and what you did, what happened and
  what you expected.
- **The CTR2-Max itself or Lynovation's firmware** (registration, its own
  screens, its settings): contact [Lynovation](https://lynovation.com).
- **AetherSDR's CTR2 window or relay:** AetherSDR's
  [issues](https://github.com/aethersdr/AetherSDR/issues).
- **Security issues:** never in a public issue; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

AetherCTR2 is maintained by volunteers. There is no guaranteed response time,
but a report with the versions and a log gets looked at first.

Everyone here follows the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
